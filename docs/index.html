How the scores work

Scores come from hand-written rules, not yet learned from flights or calibrated. Each rule scores 0-100% smoothly and the day's score is set by the weakest rule. Good is 65% or more, marginal 35-64%, poor under 35%. Thermal strength is an estimate from sunshine and boundary-layer depth. The hourly strips show the XC and acro scores for each hour. Days 4-6 are marked lower confidence because forecasts get less reliable further out. Averages of 3 models; model runs: ECMWF 18Z, GFS 18Z, ICON 18Z. Forecast data: Open-Meteo. The AI note is experimental and never changes the scores.
