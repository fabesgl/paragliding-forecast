# Decisions

Newest at the bottom. Add a line whenever we decide something. Format: date — decision — why.

- 2026-11-25 — **Day score = weakest rule** (not the product of rules). One deal-breaker should sink the day, and many mild ones shouldn't multiply it to nothing.
- 2026-11-25 — **Rule limits live in `sites/bright/rules.yaml`**, commented and validated, so Fabian can edit numbers without touching code.
- 2026-11-25 — **Acro gust limit = launch limit** (25 km/h, 0% at 40), same as XC. It is a launch limit; air turbulence is covered by the shear and westerly rules.
- 2026-11-25 — **Northerly wind aloft is a mild penalty for acro** (max −30%, never a no-go). It drifts pilots back over the hill, so they can't train as low; the effect shrinks when climbs are high.
- 2026-11-25 — **Westerlies aloft penalised** (W/WNW fully, WSW half) for turbulence off Mt Buffalo.
- 2026-11-25 — **Valley wind vs southerly**: a southerly at launch is OK when the thermic valley wind (north) is stronger. But a strong southerly at 850 hPa (>25 km/h) is a no-go that the valley wind can't override. The 6 km/h per m/s thermal factor is a default; calibration reports only and never changes rules by itself.
- 2026-11-25 — **No rain scores 100%** (rain eases the score down from 0.2 mm, 0% at 2 mm).
- 2026-11-25 — **AI second opinion never changes scores**; it only comments. Its hard limits (rain, launch wind, gusts, 850 hPa wind) force "poor".
- 2026-11-25 — **Chat labels are weak evidence**: stored at low weight, one person's reports in a day are shared out, only labels kept (no message text or names). Processed with a local model by default; Claude API is optional with a cost cap.
- 2026-11-25 — **Daily job runs after the newest ECMWF/GFS/ICON runs** (07:45 and 19:45 Melbourne) and records which runs were used. Each model is fetched separately; if one fails the others carry on and the dashboard says so.
- 2026-11-25 — **Forecast 6 days**: first 3 open, days 4–6 collapsed and marked lower confidence.
- 2026-11-25 — **The daily job owns `docs/`, `forecast_archive/`, `predictions/` etc.**; laptop commits must not include them (avoids conflicts).
- 2026-11-25 — **No XContest scraping.** Only pages Fabian saves by hand (his own login, normal browsing) are read, offline.

- 2026-11-26 — **Months where saved XContest flights < XContest's total are "incomplete"**; days with no flights there are "unknown", not "nobody flew". Counted by UTC month, as XContest filters.
- 2026-11-26 — **Tandem (glider class T) stored but excluded from XC grading** (`xc_flight_graded`).

- 2026-11-26 — **IGC tracks are imported offline** into `data/igc_flights.sqlite` (`igc_flight`, `igc_thermal`); `igc_files/` and `*.igc` are git-ignored, originals never modified. - 2026-11-26 — **IGC flight detection**: moving runs only; runs at car speed (75th percentile > 54 km/h) or that climb 100+ m and never descend (the drive up to launch) are dropped; under 2 min is not a flight. Dates and times are Australia/Melbourne local.
- 2026-11-26 — **Thermals = circling >= 360 degrees in one direction.** Tagged `climb` (avg >= 0.3 m/s) or `circling` (no lift: spirals, ridge turns); only `climb` counts as a thermal. Climb rate is the average over the whole circling segment.
- 2026-11-26 — **Launch site by coordinates** (`sites/launches.yaml`, nearest within radius), otherwise `unknown@LAT,LON` on a ~1 km grid. Flowerdale and Manilla positions are approximate (taken from where tracks start); Fabian to confirm.
- 2026-11-26 — **GPS altitude glitches** are despiked; climb rates outside -20..+15 m/s are ignored; flights with glitches are counted in `alt_glitches` (their max altitude/gain are less reliable). The same flight saved twice counts once via `igc_flight_unique`.
- 2026-11-26 — **Pilot identity for tracks = the folder** (`igc_files/pilot1/`, `pilot2/`...), hashed as `pilot_hash(salt, "igc-folder:<name>")`. Names inside IGC/GPX files are no longer read. Old rows (hashed from the header name) were re-keyed to `pilot1/<file>` by `migrate_legacy`.
- 2026-11-26 — **Track dedupe**: same path skipped; same file content for the same pilot skipped (moved/renamed copies); the same flight saved as IGC and GPX counts once in `igc_flight_unique` (IGC wins). Tracks are keyed by path under `igc_files/`, so one file name in two pilot folders is two flights.
- 2026-11-26 — **GPX is read with pattern matching, not an XML parser** (no entity expansion from untrusted files); `<trkpt>` with a time only; route-only/task files are skipped. **Sample-rate flag**: `low_rate=1` if the median gap between fixes in the airborne part is over 5 s, or over 20% of the time is in gaps over 10 s. Flagged flights are still stored, but analyses leave them out.
- 2026-11-26 — **Emily/Marcus thermal zones** (Fabian's definition): only climbs in the first 15 min within 250 m of launch; within 50 m of launch = "merged over launch" (reported separately); 50-250 m = Emily (east) / Marcus (west); the hotspot ~370 m NNW of launch is the "NNW spot" and excluded. - 2026-11-26 — **Forecast error vs BARRA-C2** (`scripts/forecast_error.py`, report `scripts/forecast_error_report.py`): 11:00-16:00 local, error = forecast - BARRA-C2. Models compared on the same hours. BARRA-C2 is itself a model, not truth.

BARRA's 10 m wind reads 22-43% weaker than the station anemometers.
