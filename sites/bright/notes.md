Local knowledge for Mystic, Bright, from an experienced acro pilot (still being refined with other pilots' input). The scoring numbers that follow from these notes live in rules.yaml.

Site layout
- Mystic faces north. The acro box is on the north side of Mystic.
- Acro pilots climb in thermals, fly into the box to do acro, then need to get back to the thermal.

Wind at acro height (about 1,000-2,000 m up)
- Best for acro: nil wind, or a light southerly (under about 10 km/h). A light southerly pushes pilots into the box; nil wind lets them stay over it.
- A northerly at height is WORSE for acro (unlike XC). It drifts pilots south, away from the box and back over the hill. It does not stop acro: it only means pilots cannot train as low. The effect is smaller when climbs are high (plenty of working height) and larger when climbs are low.
- A westerly at any height up to about 2,000 m is worse: it brings turbulence off Mt Buffalo (roughly WNW of Mystic). W and WNW count fully, WSW about half. Flyable but rough, so a clear penalty, not a no-go.

Launch
- Launch needs a north valley wind (roughly NW to NE) or near calm. NW on launch is normal and is not a problem.
- A southerly aloft is fine as long as launch still has a north valley breeze or is near calm.

Air quality
- Smooth air between thermals is ideal for acro. Thermic air is fine, but not as good. Very strong or rough thermals are a mild negative, not a no-go.

Height
- More height is better. A higher thermal top or cloud base above launch is better; days when climbs stay low are worse for acro.
